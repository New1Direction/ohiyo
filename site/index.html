Flip the lock. Now it's just you and them.

Turn on the lock in a DM or a group chat. From then on, your messages are scrambled on your device before they are sent. Our server only stores the scrambled version, so we can't read them. Neither can anyone who breaks into our server.

  • LockedDMs and group chats, once you turn the lock on. One-to-one chats use the Signal Protocol.
  • Not lockedChannels inside a space. Our server can read those, the same as most chat apps.
  • Still newLocks on group chats are experimental. A message sent while you were offline can be missed.

Our mission

Your group chat should belong to you.

Not to an ad company. Not to whoever buys the app next year.

So Ohiyo is free. The code is open for anyone to read. And if you'd rather not rely on us at all, you can run your own copy.

That's the whole plan.

Free. No catch.

  • No ads.

    We don't run ads and we don't sell your data.

  • No paid tier.

    Custom emoji, themes and GIF avatars are just part of the app.

  • No lock-in.

Quick answers.

Short ones. We'd rather tell you than have you guess.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads and there is no paid tier.

Can you read my messages?

Locked DMs and group chats: no. Channels inside a space: yes, our server can, like most chat apps. The lock tells you which is which.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Ohiyo runs in your browser, on a computer or a phone. A new desktop app is on the way.

What's a space?

Your group's home. It holds your channels, your voice rooms and your people. On Discord it would be called a server.
